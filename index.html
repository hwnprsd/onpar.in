01  /  The practice

Three things we do. Nothing else.

01→ Embed

Embed

Our engineers sit inside your team, on your repo, on your on-call rotation. They inherit your constraints instead of abstracting them away.

  • On-site or in-timezone
  • Your stack
  • Your security posture
02→ Build

Build

Retrieval, evals, orchestration, guardrails, observability. The unglamorous eighty percent between a working prompt and a system your CFO trusts.

  • Eval harnesses
  • Agent runtimes
  • Data pipelines
  • Governance
03→ Transfer

Transfer

Every engagement ends with your engineers owning the system. Documentation, runbooks, and a team that no longer needs us. That is the metric.

  • Runbooks
  • Pairing
  • Exit criteria
02  /  The engagement

Twelve weeks to production. Then we leave.

Week 00–01Discover

Read the ground truth

We read your data, not your roadmap. Two engineers, one week, a written diagnosis of where the model will actually break.

OutputDeployment thesis
Week 02–05Prove

Ship the thin slice

One workflow end-to-end, in your environment, with real users. Evals from day one so "it works" is a number, not a feeling.

OutputThin slice in prod
Week 06–10Harden

Make it boring

Failure modes, fallbacks, cost ceilings, audit trails. We chase the long tail until the pager stops going off.

OutputSLOs met, 30 days
Week 11–12Transfer

Hand over the keys

Your team runs it. We pair, document, and step back. If you still need us at week thirteen, we did it wrong.

OutputOwned in-house

Most AI initiatives fail in the last mile, where the model meets legacy data, real users, and a compliance team with veto power. That mile is our entire business.

03  /  How we work

Built for enterprises that are AI-native, not AI-curious.

We don't run workshops. We don't write strategy memos. If your organisation has already decided that models are core infrastructure, we're the people who make that decision true.

A

Senior only

Every forward deployed engineer has shipped production ML before joining. Median tenure in the field: nine years. No bench, no juniors learning on your budget.

B

Model-agnostic, opinionated

We've deployed on every frontier provider and a dozen open-weight models. We'll tell you which one is wrong for your problem, and why, in writing.

C

Evals before features

The first artifact we ship is a benchmark of your real task. Every subsequent decision is measured against it. Vibes are not a deployment strategy.

D

Your infra, your data

Nothing leaves your perimeter. We work inside your VPC, under your IAM, on your keys. We've cleared security review at banks, hospitals, and a defence prime.

E

Fixed scope, fixed exit

Engagements are priced per outcome with a written exit criterion. We are structurally incentivised to make ourselves unnecessary.

05  /  Principles

Eight years. Six rules.

01

Production is the only demo.

If it isn't handling real traffic under real constraints, it hasn't been built yet.

02

Read the data before the deck.

The failure mode is always in the data. The roadmap is never where the problem lives.

03

Measure or it didn't happen.

Every claim has an eval behind it. Every eval is one your team can rerun without us.

04

Boring beats clever.

The system your on-call engineer can debug at 3am is the system that stays deployed.

05

Own the last mile.

Integration, permissions, edge cases, politics. We don't hand those off. They're the job.

06

Leave.

Success is the day your team doesn't need to call us. We plan for it from day one.
